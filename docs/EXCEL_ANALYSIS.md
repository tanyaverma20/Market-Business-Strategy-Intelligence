## 2. Workbook Structure & Tab Inventory

The workbook contains **7 dedicated sheets**:

### 2.1 `Executive_KPI_Summary`
- **Purpose**: Provides a centralized executive scorecard of all verified market benchmarks.
- **Key Metrics**: Total Products (42), Active OEMs (11), Average Price (₹133,371), Median Price (₹126,141), Average Battery (3.72 kWh), Average Range (158.4 km), Average Speed (87.8 km/h), Range Efficiency (₹839.95/km), and Catalog HHI.
- **Audit Columns**: Value, Unit, Verification Status (`verified`), Source Dataset, Calculation Date, and Calculation Methodology.

### 2.2 `Competitive_Benchmark`
- **Purpose**: Granular model-by-model technical and commercial benchmarking table.
- **Coverage**: 42 commercial models across 11 verified OEMs (Ola, Ather, TVS, Bajaj, Ampere, Hero VIDA, Simple Energy, Revolt Motors, Ultraviolette Automotive, Kinetic Green, BGauss).
- **Metrics**: Ex-showroom price, battery capacity (kWh), certified range (km), top speed (km/h), motor power (kW), price-per-km, price-per-kWh, manufacturer rank, product rank, and price-efficiency rank.

### 2.3 `Pricing_Analysis`
- **Purpose**: Price distribution analytics, quartiles, percentiles, and market premium variance.
- **Metrics**: Price percentile (0.00 to 1.00), price quartile (Q1 to Q4), market average benchmark, OEM average benchmark, relative price position classification (`below market average`, `above market average`, `unpriced / pending`), and price premium/discount percentage.

### 2.4 `Product_Positioning`
- **Purpose**: Multi-dimensional segmentation across price, range, battery, and speed brackets.
- **Segmentation Attributes**:
  - `price_bucket`: Budget (< ₹100k), Mid-Market (₹100k–₹180k), Premium (> ₹180k)
  - `range_bucket`: Short-Range (< 120km), Standard-Range (120–180km), Long-Range (> 180km)
  - `battery_bucket`: Compact (< 3.0kWh), Standard (3.0–4.5kWh), High (> 4.5kWh)
  - `performance_bucket`: Entry (< 70km/h), Balanced (70–90km/h), High-Performance (> 90km/h)
  - `positioning_summary`: Combined cluster classification (e.g., `mid-market / standard-range`).

### 2.5 `Strategic_Recommendations`
- **Purpose**: Evidence-based strategic action plan covering Market Entry, Competitive Whitespace, Pricing, Powertrain, and Geographic Expansion.
- **Structure**: Every recommendation is linked to supporting KPIs, underlying data tables, confidence level, and strategic risk/limitation.

### 2.6 `Source_Register`
- **Purpose**: Data source inventory documenting every file, publisher, retrieval date, granularity, and provenance class.

### 2.7 `Assumptions_Provenance`
- **Purpose**: Compliance and data integrity audit sheet explicitly separating verified primary source facts, scenario inputs, and pending market data.
